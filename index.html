Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

ReviewSend ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Business name, email address, and contact information
  • Customer names and phone numbers entered into the platform
  • Message history and usage data
  • Payment information (processed securely by third-party processors)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the ReviewSend platform
  • Send SMS review requests on behalf of business users
  • Communicate with you about your account and our services
  • Comply with legal obligations

SMS Messaging

ReviewSend sends SMS messages on behalf of business users to their customers. Message and data rates may apply. Customers can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never shared with or sold to third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating the platform, including SMS delivery providers and cloud infrastructure services. All third parties are required to maintain the confidentiality of your information.

Google Business Profile Data

When a business owner connects their Google Business Profile to ReviewSend, we access their business name, star rating, review count, and review content solely to display reputation analytics within the ReviewSend dashboard. This Google data is never shared with third parties, never sold, and never used for advertising purposes. Users can revoke access at any time by disconnecting their Google account in the ReviewSend Settings tab.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at support.reviewsend@gmail.com.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support.reviewsend@gmail.com.

SMS Opt-Out Policy

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

ReviewSend is committed to respecting the preferences of all message recipients. This policy explains how recipients can opt out of receiving SMS messages sent through our platform.

How to Opt Out

To stop receiving SMS messages from any business using ReviewSend, simply reply STOP to any message you receive. You will immediately be removed from that business's messaging list and will not receive any further messages.

Confirmation

Upon receiving your STOP request, you will receive a one-time confirmation message verifying that you have been unsubscribed. No further messages will be sent.

Re-subscribing

If you wish to resume receiving messages, you may reply START at any time.

Help

For help, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at support.reviewsend@gmail.com.

Message Frequency

ReviewSend sends review request messages on a per-visit basis. Message frequency depends on how often you visit a participating business. Message and data rates may apply.

If you have any questions or concerns about SMS messaging, please contact us at support.reviewsend@gmail.com.
