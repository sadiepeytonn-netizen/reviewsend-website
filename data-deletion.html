Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

ReviewSend LLC lets businesses connect their Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, and Google Business Profile so their account manager can publish posts for them. This page explains how to delete the data we hold, including data we received from Facebook and Instagram.

Option 1: Remove ReviewSend from Facebook

  • On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  • Open Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  • Find ReviewSend, click Remove, and choose to send a data deletion request.

Facebook notifies us automatically. You will receive a confirmation code and a link where you can check the status of your request.

Option 2: Disconnect inside ReviewSend

In the ReviewSend app, open Settings → Connected accounts and click Disconnect next to Facebook & Instagram (or Google). We delete the stored access tokens right away and cancel any scheduled posts that have not been published yet.

Option 3: Email us

Email support@reviewsend.io with the subject "Data deletion request" from the email address on your account. Tell us whether you want only your Facebook and Instagram data deleted or your whole ReviewSend account.

What gets deleted

  • Facebook and Instagram access tokens, Page and account IDs and names, and your Facebook user ID
  • Records of posts ReviewSend published, and any scheduled posts not yet published
  • If you ask us to close your account: your business profile, uploaded photos, drafts, customer contact lists, and message history

We complete every request within 30 days and confirm by email. We may keep billing records where the law requires it.

What stays on Facebook, Instagram, and Google

Posts that were already published remain on your Page, Instagram account, or Business Profile, because they belong to you on those platforms. You can delete them there at any time.

Questions

Email support@reviewsend.io. See also our Privacy Policy.
